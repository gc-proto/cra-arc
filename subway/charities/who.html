Considerations

  1. Understand your legal obligation

    When applying to become a registered charity, your organization must meet all the requirements under the Income Tax Act. 

    The Federal Court of AppealFootnote 1  has confirmed that the burden of proof rests with the applicant to demonstrate that these legal requirements are satisfied. 

    We created a checklist below to help you get started. However, it does not cover every legal requirement. It is only a starting point, and meeting all the requirements listed does not mean your organization will be automatically registered. 

    You will only know whether your organization qualifies for registration after we complete a thorough analysis of your application.

    Do not apply if you aren't ready

    Before you consider applying, you should know that becoming a registered charity is a serious commitment.

    Once registered, you are expected to meet all legal requirements to maintain that status. Losing your registration can result in serious consequences, such as having to pay revocation tax on all your organization's assets.

    If you aren’t sure about your decision, you should not apply for registration.

  2. Meet the basic requirements

    To be considered for registration, your organization must meet all the following requirements:

    Being a resident in Canada

    Your organization must be established and operated in Canada.

    If it’s resident outside Canada (for example, if it's established under foreign legislation), then it is not eligible for registration.

    Being established and operated exclusively for charitable purposes

    The Supreme Court of CanadaFootnote 2  explains that your organization must exist solely to further its charitable purposes.

    If it’s established to generate profit or pursue non-charitable purposes, then it is not eligible.

    To learn more: Charitable purposes and activities.

    Devoting resources exclusively to charitable activities

    The Supreme Court of CanadaFootnote 2  explains that your organization must use all of its resources—including funds, personnel, and property—for activities that further its charitable purposesFootnote 3 .

    Using resources for non-charitable purposes will make your organization ineligible for registration.

    Submit a complete application

    You must answer every question on the application form as completely and thoroughly as possible, especially when describing your organization's activities.

    You must also submit all necessary supporting documents and ensure your governing documents are complete and up to date.
