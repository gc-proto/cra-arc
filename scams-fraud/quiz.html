Question 5 of 14

False or misleading information could be described as misinformation or disinformation.

What is the best description for each of these terms?

  • a) Misinformation is false information shared by mistake, while disinformation is false information shared on purpose
  • b) Misinformation and disinformation are the same thing
  • c) Misinformation only happens online and disinformation only happens in the news
  • d) Disinformation is harmless, while misinformation is false information that causes harm

That's correct

Sorry, that's incorrect

The correct answer is: a) Misinformation is false information shared by mistake, while disinformation is false information shared on purpose

Misinformation is false information that someone shares without realizing it’s wrong. Disinformation is false information that is deliberately created or shared to mislead people.

Both misinformation and disinformation can happen across various platforms, including online, in the news, or in conversations.

That's correct

The correct answer is: d) Options a and c

Official Government of Canada websites are available in English and French and usually begin with canada.ca or end with .gc.ca. The "dot" before .ca or .gc.ca is important. Fake websites can include extra words or use hyphens instead such as -gc-ca to look legitimate.
